Privacy notice

Updated 5 November 2026

mind-the-dag is a workflow diagram editor maintained by Haotian Zheng. This notice explains how the app uses information when you try it locally or join its cloud workspace.

Signing in

Google and GitHub provide your account identifier, verified email address and basic profile information, such as your name and available profile picture. We use this information to identify your account and show your name to collaborators. Email registration uses the name, email and password you provide; the server stores a password hash.

Google sign-in requests only your basic profile and email address. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive or your contacts. You can manage Google's permission in your Google Account connections.

When you first use another sign-in provider, the same verified email identifies the same workspace account. Different emails create separate accounts, subject to an invitation. An already connected Google or GitHub identity continues to identify its original account if its provider email changes.

Maps and collaboration

Without signing in, drafts stay in your browser. When you sign in, browser drafts can be moved into your cloud account. The cloud workspace stores your maps, group memberships, invitations and editing information on its PocketBase server hosted on CSC Rahti. Personal maps are available to their owner; group maps are available to members of that group.

A view-only map link contains the map itself. Anyone you give that link to can view it and save a copy.

Browser storage and service providers

The app uses browser storage for drafts, preferences and your sign-in session. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Google or GitHub handles its own sign-in process. These hosting and authentication services may process connection information, such as IP addresses, under their own policies. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics scripts.

Retention and requests

Cloud account and map data remain stored until removed. Operational backups may retain earlier copies until they rotate out. Browser-only drafts can be removed in the app or by clearing this site's browser data.

For account data access, corrections, deletion requests or privacy questions, contact haotian.zheng@helsinki.fi.
